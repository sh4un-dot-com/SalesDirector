## Tips for Using These Prompts

1. **Copy the entire prompt block** including all product details — the AI needs this context.
2. **Customize the target audience** — swap "B2B sales teams" for your specific vertical (e.g., "SaaS startups," "real estate brokerages," "insurance agencies").
3. **Add screenshots** — after generating text, pair with actual SalesDirector screenshots for highest-impact collateral.
4. **Iterate** — ask the AI to "make it more urgent," "shorten by 30%," or "add a testimonial section" to refine.
5. **Brand consistency** — always include "Akita Engineering · Niagara Falls, Canada · www.akitaengineering.com."
